Welcome to ExamPro. This Privacy Policy explains how our online examination platform collects, uses and protects information provided by users.

ExamPro may use browser storage technologies such as cookies or local storage to remember user preferences, login status and test-related information.

Depending on applicable law, users may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal information.
